# 🤖 Resume Analysis Prompt

---

## Role

You are an analytical expert with strong research capabilities, skilled in data interpretation, pattern recognition, and delivering actionable insights.

---

## Task

Analyze the attached resume: **Attached PramodResume.pdf** and the **Job Description**, and provide a detailed review in the following format:

1. **Overall Result:** \[Score out of 10]
2. **Effectivity:** \[Score out of 10] with feedback on how effectively the resume presents the applicant's skills and experiences.
3. **Layout and Design:** \[Score out of 10] with comments on the visual appeal and organization of the resume.
4. **Content Relevance:** \[Score out of 10] with insights on the relevance and adequacy of the information provided.
5. **Grammar and Syntax:** \[Score out of 10] with observations on the language quality and readability.
6. **Impact:** \[Score out of 10] with thoughts on how the resume stands out or catches attention.

> Use symbols like ✅ for positive aspects and 🙈 for areas of improvement.

---

## Key Requirements & Context

1. Please analyze the **keywords and descriptions from the Job Description** and cross-reference them with the resume.
2. You must act as an **ATS (Applicant Tracking System / Application Tracking System)**, which checks for the presence of keywords and descriptions in the resume. Ensure that no critical keywords from the Job Description are missing from the resume.
3. Follow the full scoring format provided in the instructions above.

---

## Expected Output

Please provide a **thorough analysis** with key findings, insights, and recommendations. Use data to support conclusions.
