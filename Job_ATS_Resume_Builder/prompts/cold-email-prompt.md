# Cold Email Generation Prompt

## Inputs

1. **Job Description:** `${jd}`
2. **Resume Details:** `${resume}`

## Task

Your task is to analyze the provided job description and resume thoroughly. Identify the **Company Name** from the job description. If a specific recruiter or hiring manager is not mentioned, address the emails to the **Company's HR Department** or **Hiring Team** at [Company Name]. 

Create a series of five cold emails where each email serves a specific purpose:

- **Email 1: Introduction and Expression of Interest**
  Introduce the candidate, express their interest in the position at the company, and briefly mention how their skills align with the job requirements.

- **Email 2: Highlighting Relevant Experience**
  Focus on one or two key experiences from the resume that directly relate to the job description, explaining how these experiences make the candidate a great fit for the company.

- **Email 3: Discussing Skills and Achievements**
  Elaborate on the candidate's skills and significant achievements, especially those that are relevant to the job description and the company's goals.

- **Email 4: Addressing Company Values and Culture**
  Explain why the candidate is attracted to the company's culture and values, and how they see themselves contributing to the team.

- **Email 5: Closing and Call to Action**
  Summarize the key points, reiterate interest in the position, and propose a follow-up action, like a meeting or phone call with the HR team.

## Requirements

Please ensure that each email is:

- **STRICTLY Addressed to HR:** You MUST address the email to the Company's HR Department or Hiring Team (e.g., "Dear [Company Name] HR Team," or "Dear Hiring Manager at [Company Name],")
- Professionally written
- Concise and short
- Tailored to both the resume and the job description
- Written in a friendly yet professional tone
